Section 01 — The Big Picture

From 288,000 to 845,000 in 20 years

Australia's international student population has nearly tripled since 2005. COVID-19 triggered the sharpest collapse on record, yet by 2024 enrolments hit an all-time high.

Total International Student Enrolments, 2005–2025
Key insight: COVID-19 closed Australia's borders in March 2020. Enrolments dropped 24% over two years, then rebounded faster than any prior recovery. The 2024 peak of 850,921 surpassed the pre-pandemic high by over 12%.

Source: Australian Department of Education, International Student Data 2025

Education as Australia's 4th-Largest Export, 2024

Source: ABS, International Trade Statistics 2024. AUD millions.

Domestic vs. Overseas Student Enrolments, 2015–2024
Key insight: By 2024, overseas students reached nearly 54% of the domestic total, showing how internationally embedded Australia's higher education has become.

Source: Australian Department of Education, Higher Education Statistics 2024

Section 02 — Where They Come From

A Global Classroom, Led by Asia

Over 150 countries send students to Australia. The vast majority come from Asia, but the balance is shifting fast as India and Nepal surge.

Flow of International Students to Australia, 2025
Key insight: China, India, and Nepal together account for over 51% of all international enrolments. Southeast and South Asian corridors dominate, with Latin America growing rapidly.

Source: Australian Department of Education, International Student Data 2025. Line thickness and dot size proportional to student numbers.

Top 10 Source Countries, 2025
Key insight: China and India together account for over 43% of all enrolments. Nepal has rapidly emerged as a notable third source country.

Source: Australian Department of Education, International Student Data 2025

Section 03 — Where They Live and Study

Victoria and NSW host two-thirds

International students are concentrated in Australia's two largest states. The Group of Eight universities attract the most students and are most financially dependent on their fees.

Section 04 — What They Study

Management dominates. IT is surging.

Management and Commerce is the most popular field at every level. Information Technology has seen the fastest growth, up 22% in a single year.

International Students by Field and Level of Course, 2024
Key insight: Management dominates at Bachelor and Postgraduate Coursework levels. IT skews heavily postgraduate, reflecting demand for skilled tech workers seeking migration pathways. White cells indicate near-zero enrolments.

Source: Australian Department of Education, Table 7.2 — All Overseas Students by Broad Level of Course and Field, 2024

Section 05 — What Happens After

Studying here is a pathway to staying

For many students, a degree is just the beginning. Australia's migration program is closely intertwined with its education sector.

Why Students Choose to Stay in Australia
Key insight: Career opportunities and post-study work rights rank highest. Proximity to home country ranks lowest.

Source: Study Australia International Student Survey 2023.

Migration Pathways, 2023–24
Key insight: The Skilled stream accounts for over 60% of permanent visa grants — the primary pathway for former international students.

Source: Department of Home Affairs, Permanent Migration Program Outcomes 2023–24
