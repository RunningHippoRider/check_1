The World Comes to Study

Australia hosts the world's third-highest share of international students per capita. This is the story of who comes, where they come from, what they study, and where they end up.

Section 01: The Big Picture

From 288,000 to 845,000 in 20 years

Australia's international student population has nearly tripled since 2005. COVID-19 triggered the sharpest collapse on record, yet by 2024 enrolments hit an all-time high.

Total International Student Enrolments, 2005–2025
Key insight: COVID-19 closed Australia's borders in March 2020. Enrolments dropped 24% over two years, then rebounded faster than any prior recovery. The 2024 peak of 850,921 surpassed the pre-pandemic high by over 12%.
Education as Australia's 4th-Largest Export, 2024
Key insight: Education contributed over AUD $51B to Australia's economy in 2024, ranking as the 4th-largest export. International students pay tuition fees around three times higher than domestic students, making each enrolment a significant economic contribution.
Domestic vs. Overseas Student Enrolments, 2015–2024
Key insight: By 2024, overseas students reached nearly 54% of the domestic total, showing how internationally embedded Australia's higher education has become.
Section 02: Where They Come From

A Global Classroom, Led by Asia

Over 150 countries send students to Australia. The vast majority come from Asia, but the balance is shifting fast as India and Nepal surge.

Flow of International Students to Australia, 2025
Key insight: China, India, and Nepal together account for over 51% of all international enrolments. Southeast and South Asian corridors dominate, with Latin America growing rapidly.
Top 10 Source Countries, 2025
Key insight: China and India together account for over 43% of all enrolments. Nepal has rapidly emerged as a notable third source country.
Top 10 Source Countries: Race, 2005–2025
2005
Key insight: China peaked around 2017. India's rise has been relentless since 2015. Nepal entered the top 3 only after 2018.
Student Visa Applications vs. Grants by Nationality, 2024-25

Ordered by number of applications lodged

Visa lodgements capture applications received by the Department of Home Affairs.
Visa grants capture approved lodgements. Grant totals may exceed lodgements in a period because processing spans multiple months.
Key insight: India's 74% grant rate is the lowest among major applicant countries. Philippines drops to 79%. Colombia shows a 120% grant rate, meaning more visas were granted than applications lodged that year, because processing times vary: grants finalized this period may include backlogs from prior applications.
Section 03: Where They Study

Victoria and NSW host two-thirds

International students are concentrated in Australia's two largest states. The Group of Eight universities attract the most students and are most financially dependent on their fees.

International Students per 1,000 Residents by State, 2024
Key insight: Normalised by population, the ACT has the highest concentration of international students relative to residents, followed closely by Victoria.
Go8 Universities: Total vs. International Enrolments, 2024
Key insight: Bubble size = international students enrolled. University of Sydney and Monash lead in international headcount.
Go8 University Revenue Breakdown, 2023

International students are far more profitable than domestic ones

Key insight: International student fees are the single largest revenue category at Sydney (AUD 1.4B) and Melbourne (AUD 993M). Domestic HELP and HECS contributions are a fraction of international fee income at most Go8 universities.
Section 04: What They Study

Management dominates. IT is surging.

Management and Commerce is the most popular field at every level. Information Technology has seen the fastest growth, up 22% in a single year.

International Students by Field and Level of Course, 2024
Key insight: Management dominates at Bachelor and Postgraduate Coursework levels. IT skews heavily postgraduate, reflecting demand for skilled tech workers seeking migration pathways.
Graduate Outcomes: Skills and Workplace Readiness

International student survey 2023, Study Australia

Key insight: Over 90% of graduates rate their English, problem-solving and self-management skills as strong. Nearly all credit personal skills and language proficiency as key to securing employment.
Section 05: What Happens After

Studying here is a pathway to staying

For many students, a degree is just the beginning. Australia's migration program is closely intertwined with its education sector.

What Happens a Decade Later: Education Type to Residency Outcome, 2010–11 Cohort

Tracking 362,830 international students enrolled in 2010–11 through to 2021–22

Key insight: Studying in Australia is a major pathway to staying. Over half (55%) of the 2010–11 cohort remained in Australia a decade later. VET students were more likely to gain permanent residence (56%) than Higher Education students (45%), showing study as a viable and highly successful migration route.
Migration Pathways, 2023-24
Key insight: The Skilled stream accounts for over 60% of permanent visa grants, the primary pathway for former international students.
